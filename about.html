— § 01
Our Story

Two kinds of contractors. We wanted to be a third.

"The work we do today is the foundation for the home you'll live in for the next thirty years. We don't take that lightly."

Cornerstone Renovations was founded by a crew lead who spent years watching how exterior renovation work actually gets done in Portland — and saw room for something better. Smaller companies often cut corners to stay competitive. Larger ones layered on overhead until the bid no longer reflected the work.

We started Cornerstone to do it differently. A locally family-owned crew with decades of combined experience. Premium materials installed exactly the way the manufacturers designed. Warranties that mean something. And a name on every job that we're proud to stand behind.

Today, we serve homeowners across the Portland metro and southwest Washington — building roofs, siding, windows, gutters, and decks engineered to last through the next thirty Pacific Northwest winters.

— § 02 / What We Stand On

Four principles.
Built into every job.

i.

Specialized Installation

Every flashing detail, every fastener pattern, every weather barrier — installed to spec, every time. Manufacturer training is built into our process because the warranty is only as good as the installer behind it. We don't believe in shortcuts.

ii.

Lifetime Products

We install only premium, manufacturer-backed materials from names like GAF, James Hardie, Andersen, Milgard, Simonton, and Trex. Built to outlast trends, weather, and ownership. The warranty is the receipt of the install.

iii.

Locally Family Owned

Cornerstone is family-owned and operated, proudly serving the Portland metro and southwest Washington. Our name is on every job, and we treat every home like it belongs to a neighbor — because most of the time, it does.

iv.

Affordable Solutions

We offer itemized bids and flexible financing options to fit any budget. We don't believe cost should stop you from taking care of your home. When the bid lands in your inbox, every line is a real number for real work.
