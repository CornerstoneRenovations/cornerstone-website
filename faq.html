— Frequently Asked Questions

Answers, before the sit-down.

The most common questions homeowners ask before scheduling their consultation.

Are estimates really free?+
Yes. Every consultation includes a no-cost, no-obligation walkthrough of your home and a detailed quote for your project. You only pay if you decide to move forward.
Where do you work?+
We serve homeowners across the Portland metro area and southwest Washington — including Portland, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Lake Oswego, Tigard, Gresham, Vancouver, and surrounding communities.
Are you licensed and insured?+
Yes. Cornerstone Renovations is fully licensed (Oregon CCB #261422), bonded, and insured. We carry all the coverage required to work on Oregon and Washington homes — and a little more, just in case.
What manufacturers do you partner with?+
We work exclusively with leading manufacturers including GAF, James Hardie, Andersen, Milgard, Simonton, Premier Gutter Cover, and Trex. Certifications and partnerships with manufacturers like these aren't easy to earn — and they ensure our installs qualify for the strongest available warranties.
Do you offer financing?+
Yes. We offer flexible financing options through trusted third-party lenders so you can break the project into manageable monthly payments. We'll walk through your options during your consultation.
Do you handle insurance claims?+
We do. If your project involves storm damage or another insurable event, our team will work directly with your adjuster to advocate for full scope of repair — so you don't have to navigate the claim alone.
How long do projects take?+
Every project is different. A roof replacement may be one to three days. A full siding job may run two to three weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline as part of your custom proposal.
What kind of warranty do I get?+
Every Cornerstone project is backed by manufacturer warranties on materials plus our own written workmanship guarantee. Specifics depend on the products selected for your project — we'll walk through everything during your consultation.
Do I need to be home during the install?+
No. Once your project is approved, we can manage the install with minimal homeowner presence — though you're always welcome on-site whenever you'd like to check progress.
How do I get started?+
Click the Get a Quote button at the top of this page or call us at (503) 502-5613. We'll schedule your free consultation and go from there.
